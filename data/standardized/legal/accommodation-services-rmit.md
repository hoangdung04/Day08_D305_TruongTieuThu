# Accommodation Services Rmit

QUY DINH KY TUC XA VA DICH VU HO TRO CHO O RMIT VIET NAM

1. Dich vu Cho o Sinh vien:
- Ky tuc xa RMIT Nam Sai Gon cung cap phong o hien dai voi cac lua chon: Phong don, Phong doi va Can ho 3 phong.
- Gia thue phong dao dong tu 4,500,000 VND den 9,000,000 VND / thang \(da bao gom chi phi dien, nuoc, wifi\).

2. Quy dinh an ninh va sinh hoat:
- Gio thiet quan: Ky tuc xa dong cua luc 23:00 hang dem.
- Khong su dung chat cam, thuoc la, do uong co con trong khu vuc Ky tuc xa.
- Khong dan khach la qua dem neu chua co su dong y bang van ban cua Ban Quan ly KTX.

3. Quy trinh dang ky cho o:
- Sinh vien nop don dang ky truc tuyen qua Residential Portal RMIT truoc khai giang 30 ngay.
- Dat coc 1 thang tien phong de giu cho.
